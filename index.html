Easy Setup

How to Install Recordify

Follow these simple steps to install the APK on your phone, tablet, or television.

📱

On Android Phone & Tablet

  • 1
    Download the APK

    Click the green Download for Android button above to download Recordify.apk.

  • 2
    Open the Downloaded File

    Tap the download notification or locate the APK file in your device's Downloads folder.

  • 3
    Allow Unknown Sources

    If prompted by Android, toggle Allow from this source in Settings for your browser or file manager.

  • 4
    Tap Install & Launch

    Tap Install. Once complete, open Recordify and start streaming immediately!

📺

On Android TV & Google TV

  • A
    Method 1: Downloader App (Recommended)

    Install the free Downloader app from Google Play Store on your TV. Enter the direct APK URL to download and install.

  • B
    Method 2: Send Files to TV

    Download Recordify.TV.apk on your phone. Install Send Files to TV on both phone & TV, transfer the APK over Wi-Fi, and install.

  • C
    Method 3: USB Flash Drive

    Copy Recordify.TV.apk to a USB flash drive, plug it into your TV's USB port, and open it via any TV File Manager.

  • ✓
    Launch from TV Apps Row

    Recordify TV will appear directly on your Android TV home screen apps row ready for your remote!
